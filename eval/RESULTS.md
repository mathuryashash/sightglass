# SightGlass evaluation (offline, recorded data and simulator)

Generated by `python -m agentwarden.evaluate`. Seeds are fixed, so a rerun gives the same numbers. Configuration: 200 seeds per detection setting, 200 benign runs of 30 simulated minutes, 200 synthetic incidents per class. Nothing here touched the network or a model.

## 1. Level reading quality (real data, no tape)

The recorded pour has 54 SAM readings at 1 fps from a hand-held bottle. There is no tape or scale, so the true level is not measured. The honest proxy is that the level in a steady pour never goes down. Frames 0-8 are a hand and bottle entering the shot and are left out of the checks below (45 frames analysed).

- Monotonicity: 1 backward steps after frame 8, the largest 1.8 points (0 larger than 2 points).
- Frame-to-frame noise: median |delta - local trend| = 1.20 points (local trend = median of the 4 neighbouring deltas; pairs touching a clamped 100 are left out, 37 pairs used). ## 3. False alarms

200 benign runs of 30 simulated minutes at sigma = 3 points (100.0 hours of exposure): 0 false trips, 0.000 per hour. Upper 95 % bound 0.030 per hour (rule of three (3 / hours)). The noise here is gaussian; real readings have outliers (see the entry frames above), so this is not a promise about a real camera.

| short_blind_then_spoof | 10-16 | freeze @ 12 | True | 21 | 6 | 15.6 | 100.0 |
| blind_recovers_late | 10-40 | freeze @ 12 | True | 45 | 30 | 15.6 | 100.0 |

The blind-then-spoof gap, measured: with the camera blind from t = 10 s to the end and the PLC frozen from t = 12 s, nothing trips (44 blind ticks raised the alarm). At the end the PLC says 15.6 % while the hidden camera level in the recording is 100.0 %, a gap of 84.4 points that stays undetected. If the camera comes back (blind until t = 40 s) the trip follows at t = 45 s.

## 7. Why SAM: colour-threshold baseline

A naive reader (HSV orange threshold inside the container box, lowest continuous run of rows gives the surface) on the same 54 raw frames. Both readers use the SAM container box of the same frame (auto_from_box); the colour reader has no container detector, so only the liquid-surface localisation differs. The thresholds were set by looking at one frame, not tuned per frame. Frames 9 onwards:

| reader | backward steps | of them > 2 pts | largest backward step pts | median noise pts | frames with no reading |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| SAM 3.1 | 1 | 0 | 1.8 | 1.20 | 0 |
| colour threshold | 3 | 2 | 7.9 | 1.16 | 0 |

Mean absolute difference between the two readers: 2.9 points. The comparison holds for this one clip only and says nothing about other bottles, liquids or lighting.

## Limits

- No tape ground truth: level quality uses a monotonic-pour proxy and eyeballed approximate levels, so no accuracy figure is claimed.
- One hand-held clip and one bottle with one orange liquid; the first frames show a hand entering the shot.
- The PLC, the tank and the camera noise are simulated. Detection and false-alarm numbers describe the rules against a gaussian fake camera.
- The T = 15 point threshold is the provisional default, not derived from a measured error distribution.
- The blind-then-spoof gap (section 5) is a documented limit: a camera that goes blind and then a spoof begins is not detected.
- The model results come from a handful of recorded Spark calls (section 6 gives the count), far too few for rates.
- The synthetic incidents for the rule baseline are self-authored.
- In replay the plant follows the recording, so overflow is not tested there; the claim is limited to tripping before the camera reached 95 %.
